LocaleSpec Privacy Notice

Local analysis

LocaleSpec reads the saved UTF8 JSON resource files explicitly selected inside your IDE project. It evaluates a fixed bundled FormatJS-compatible parser and rules without executing your project's configuration or build commands. The Plugin does not transmit your source code or translations to the operator or external processing services. Version one includes no telemetry, advertising tracker, hosted synchronization or automatic translation endpoint.

The local installation-integrity check reads the Plugin's own packaged engine resource. It adds no network communication and does not inspect additional customer files. Copy-resistance controls do not encrypt or lock your project data.

Local review state and exports

Source-message hashes, catalog-configuration identity and suppression reasons are stored in IDE workspace state after your explicit actions. Hashes are fingerprints that can be compared with guessed content; they are not encrypted secrets.

Exporting a report saves a file at the location you select. Reports can include paths, message IDs and diagnostic details. Review them before sharing. Translation files are not rewritten by analysis. Reports remain accessible after subscription expiry.

You control local files and exports. IDE project-state storage follows the IDE's own conventions; the actual restart and uninstall behavior must be verified before public release.

JetBrains services

IDE licensing, Marketplace purchases, update checks and services initiated by the IDE may communicate with JetBrains under its own applicable notices and terms. Local analysis is not a promise that the whole IDE or entitlement system always works offline. The developer does not receive your payment-card details through this Plugin.

Voluntary support

Support mail is handled through the owner-controlled Gmail alias above. A request may contain your email address, message and any material you voluntarily include. We use it to answer the request, diagnose sanitized reproductions, maintain the product and keep necessary support records.

Provide a minimal sanitized example; do not send confidential repositories, credentials, banking data or identity documents. Authorized online services and automated tools may process redacted support material to help prepare replies and investigate reported problems. Confidential customer projects must not be uploaded. Private customer issues are not published without permission.

Support records are kept while needed to handle the request, maintain necessary product records, meet applicable legal duties or address a related dispute. Requests concerning support data can be sent to the same product address and will be handled subject to applicable rights and necessary retention duties. This notice does not claim a particular legal jurisdiction or a verified international-transfer arrangement.

Scope and changes

This notice describes the current product boundary. Any proposed telemetry, customer-content transmission or expanded data access requires a separate review and the required approvals before implementation. No data-sale or third-party advertising feature is included.
